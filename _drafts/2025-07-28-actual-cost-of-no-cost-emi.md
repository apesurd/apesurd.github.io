I, personally, avoid EMIs on durables. Why? Because simply put I am taking a loan on a depreciating asset, and paying extra in the form of interest.

But at the time of writing this, I see an increasing trend in almost every e-commerce platform offering a no-cost EMI, NCEMI in short, option to the customers.
And they are pushing it too, with some great discounts.

So even though I am skeptical, it's worth considering if overall the EMI option is really beneficial for me.

So what are the other charges associated with NCEMI?
1. Processing fee. 
2. GSTs on interest and processing fee.

-- Summary.
The thing is that the product's cost is not only the sum of the installments of the product.

Even from your end, you are told that you are not any additional money when doing EMI, bank can't provide credit to you without interest, so at their end they are still splitting your payment into pricipal and interst.
And GST is applicable for the interest and processing fee. (as per the article in reference)

You have to consider the fee of the bank for processing the EMI. And top of that you need to consider the GST on the interest and processing fee.
